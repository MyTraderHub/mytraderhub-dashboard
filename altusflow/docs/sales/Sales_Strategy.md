# AltusFlow.ai — Sales Strategy

> Companion to `docs/sales/ICP_Guidelines.md`. Defines positioning, messaging, and conversion paths.

---

## Positioning Statement

**AltusFlow.ai** is not a website agency, ad shop, or outbound tool vendor. We sell **integrated growth engines** — autonomous systems where traffic, conversion, and outbound sourcing communicate 24/7 in the background.

**One-liner:** *We plug your leaky bucket with premium web design, native AI chat, automated traffic, and intent-based outbound.*

---

## The 3 Verticals (Sales Narrative)

### 1. The Conversion Engine — Premium AI-Powered Websites
- **Sell:** Speed, conversion design, and a custom-trained 24/7 AI chat assistant baked in
- **Differentiator:** Native chat (not Intercom/Drift bolt-on); trained on *their* business
- **Proof point:** Answers queries in ~3 seconds; qualifies and books meetings overnight

### 2. The Inbound Magnet — Meta Ads & Sales Funnels
- **Sell:** Targeted Meta campaigns + psychological funnels → booked calls, not just clicks
- **Differentiator:** Funnels wired into the native chatbot for instant qualification
- **Proof point:** Retargeting loops warm prospects back into a system that already knows how to close

### 3. The Outbound Hunter — Intent-Based Lead Sourcing
- **Sell:** AI scans LinkedIn/social for buyers posting about problems they solve; drafts hyper-personalized outreach
- **Differentiator:** Executive review workflow — no spray-and-pray; signals feed back into ads and chat
- **Proof point:** Replaces 10+ hrs/week of manual SDR research

## Sales Motion

| Stage | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **1. Identify** | Intent-based sourcing per `docs/sales/ICP_Guidelines.md` — LinkedIn, social, news, job posts |
| **2. Personalize** | 1-sentence hook tied to a specific trigger (funding, post, hire, pain signal) |
| **3. Outreach** | Hyper-personalized pitch drafted for executive review; human approves before send |
| **4. Convert** | Book strategy call → "Identify Your Revenue Gaps" intake → tailored systems analysis |
| **5. Close** | Scoped proposal based on which vertical(s) plug their biggest leaks |

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already have a website" | "Is it converting after hours? Does it qualify leads in 3 seconds without your team?" |
| "We use Intercom/Drift" | "Bolt-ons don't know your offers natively. Ours is trained on your business and wired into ads + outbound." |
| "We have an ads agency" | "Do they own conversion on the landing page and chat? We integrate traffic → qualification → close." |
| "We do outbound in-house" | "How many hours/week on research? Our AI finds intent signals and drafts pitches for your review." |
| "Too expensive" | "Compare replacing 3–4 tools + retainers. One engine, one pipeline, runs 24/7." |
| "Not ready yet" | "Fill the intake form — we'll map your gaps so you know exactly what to fix when timing is right." |

## Conversion Paths

1. **Landing page chatbot** → Answers questions → Routes to `#contact` form
2. **Contact form** → "Identify Your Revenue Gaps" → Bottleneck dropdown qualifies lead
3. **Outbound outreach** → Personalized hook → Calendar link / reply to start conversation
4. **Meta ads** → Funnel → AI chat qualifies → Books call

## Messaging Tone

- **Professional, direct, data-driven** — no hype, no jargon soup
- Lead with the prospect's pain (leaky bucket), not our features
- Use specifics: "3 seconds," "24/7," "7 PM on a Saturday," "booked calls"
- Never promise guaranteed revenue; promise systems that eliminate leaks

---

## Competitor Exclusion (Outbound)

Do not target companies that are direct competitors:

- Full-service growth agencies offering web + AI chat + paid ads + outbound in one package
- Established players with identical 3-vertical positioning

Indirect competitors (single-vertical) are fair game — position integration as the advantage.
