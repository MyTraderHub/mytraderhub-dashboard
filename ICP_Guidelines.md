# AltusFlow.ai — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Guidelines

> Reference this file for all lead sourcing, outbound, and sales qualification decisions.

## Company Overview

**AltusFlow.ai** builds fully automated growth engines for high-value B2B businesses. Our 3-vertical ecosystem combines premium AI-powered websites (with native chat), Meta ads & sales funnels, and intent-based lead sourcing into one integrated pipeline.

---

## Target Industry / Sector

| Priority | Industries |
|----------|-----------|
| **Primary** | B2B professional services, marketing agencies, management consulting, SaaS (B2B), financial advisory, legal (mid-market firms), commercial real estate |
| **Secondary** | Healthcare services (non-enterprise), IT managed services, executive coaching, fractional C-suite providers |
| **Excluded** | Low-ticket e-commerce, MLM/network marketing, gambling, adult, crypto speculation, direct AltusFlow competitors |

---

## Company Size

| Attribute | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Revenue** | $2M–$100M annual (sweet spot: $5M–$30M) |
| **Employees** | 10–500 (minimum 10 unless ACV > $50k) |
| **Marketing spend** | $3,000+/month on paid traffic, ads, or agency retainers |
| **Sales model** | Closes via booked calls, demos, or high-touch consultations — not cart checkout |

---

## Key Pain Points (Qualifying Signals)

Leads scoring highest exhibit **2+** of these:

1. **Leaky bucket** — Spending on traffic but poor conversion; leads go cold before follow-up
2. **Passive website** — Site is a brochure; no 24/7 chat or lead qualification
3. **After-hours loss** — Missing inquiries evenings/weekends; slow response times (>4 hours)
4. **Ad inefficiency** — Running Meta/Google ads with high CPC but low cost-per-booked-call
5. **Manual outbound** — SDRs or founders spending 10+ hrs/week on LinkedIn research
6. **Tool fragmentation** — Paying for separate website, chatbot, ads agency, and outbound tools with no integration
7. **Intent signals** — Recently posted about hiring marketing, scaling growth, lead gen struggles, or website redesign

---

## Decision-Maker Persona

| Role | Priority | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| **Founder / CEO** | ★★★ | Primary buyer at companies <100 employees |
| **CMO / VP Marketing** | ★★★ | Owns traffic, conversion, and marketing budget |
| **VP Sales / Head of Revenue** | ★★☆ | Cares about lead quality and SDR efficiency |
| **Head of Growth** | ★★★ | Ideal champion; understands full-funnel integration |
| **Director of Marketing** | ★★☆ | Qualify if they control budget or influence CMO |

**Disqualify:** Individual contributors, interns, HR, IT unless explicitly tasked with growth evaluation.

---

## Strict Qualification Criteria

A lead is **Qualified** only when ALL of the following are true:

1. **Revenue / spend threshold** — Company revenue >$2M OR documented marketing spend >$3k/mo
2. **Decision-maker access** — Contact is Founder, C-level, or VP-level (or confirmed budget authority)
3. **Activity signal** — Posted on LinkedIn or relevant social in the last 30 days, OR company shows a trigger event (funding, hiring, product launch, leadership change)
4. **Verified contact** — LinkedIn profile or company website URL confirmed live; no generic info@ unless sole decision-maker is verified
5. **Not on exclusion list** — See below

---

## Exclusion List

Do **not** include:

- Direct competitors (growth agencies offering integrated web + AI chat + ads + outbound)
- Businesses under 10 employees (unless ACV >$50k and verified)
- Companies with no web presence or abandoned LinkedIn (inactive 6+ months)
- Generic contact info with no named decision-maker
- Industries: gambling, adult, MLM, pure e-commerce <$100 AOV
- Government / enterprise RFP-only procurement (sales cycle >18 months)
- Businesses that explicitly state "no agencies" or "in-house only" in recent posts

---

## Intent Triggers (Personalization Hooks)

Prioritize leads showing these **recent** triggers (last 90 days):

| Trigger | Hook Angle |
|---------|-----------|
| Posted about lead gen struggles | "Saw your post on lead gen — our AI pipeline books calls while you sleep" |
| Hiring SDRs or marketers | "Scaling outbound? Our intent engine drafts personalized outreach automatically" |
| Website redesign / rebrand | "Native AI chat on launch day — not a bolt-on 6 months later" |
| Funding round | "Post-raise growth push? Integrated traffic → conversion → outbound in one system" |
| New CMO / VP Marketing hire | "New leadership, new systems — we replace 3–4 fragmented tools with one engine" |
| Complaining about ad ROI | "Meta clicks without booked calls? Our funnels + AI chat fix the leak" |
| After-hours missed leads | "Your site can't answer at 7 PM Saturday — ours does in 3 seconds" |

---

## Review Needed (Flag, Don't Auto-Include)

Flag leads for human review when:

- Revenue/spend cannot be verified but pain signals are strong
- Contact is Director-level without confirmed budget authority
- Company is borderline on employee count (8–12 employees)
- Industry is adjacent but not in primary/secondary list
- LinkedIn activity is 30–60 days old (stale but not dead)

---

## Output Standard

All qualified leads must be presented in a Markdown table:

| Company Name | Decision Maker | Job Title | LinkedIn/Website URL | Personalization Hook |

Maintain accuracy over volume. Exclude unverified data.
