Help & Support

Getting Started

Sign in with your user id, then explore Home for portfolio equity and daily P&L.

Use Watch to search tickers, Account for orders, and Journal for your trade calendar.

Watchlist & Quotes

Search any ticker on the Watch page. Tap a row to open quotes, latest news, and price alerts.

Buy and Sell buttons open the order sheet for quick paper trades.

Price Alerts

From a stock sheet, choose Above or Below, enter a target price, and tap Set.

Active alerts appear as chips. Delete with the × on each chip. Limits depend on your plan.

Orders & Account

The Account page shows portfolio value, cash, buying power, and day-trade status.

Cancel open orders from the table. Order history lists filled and cancelled trades.

Trade Journal

The Journal calendar colors each day by outcome. Tap a day with trades for details.

Monthly win rate and P&L summary appear below the grid.

Billing & Plans

Free includes limited AI chats, alerts, and analyses. Pro raises all limits.

Usage meters above reset monthly. Contact support via the AI assistant below for upgrade help.
